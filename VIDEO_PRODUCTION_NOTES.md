# Project video production notes — Version 12

All six projects now contain MP4 videos with **no audio streams**. The Model Rocket audio was stripped without recompressing the video. The other five videos were produced as narrated-free illustrated sequences with on-screen steps and accompanying VTT text tracks plus accessible written step descriptions.

The CAD Design Project video is an **illustrated reconstruction of the Fusion 360 workflow** and must not be described as footage recorded inside Autodesk Fusion. The menu concepts were checked against Autodesk Fusion Help: Create Sketch, Sketch rectangles, Solid > Create > Extrude, and Assemble > Joint. Controls may look different in a future version of Fusion. For an exact product-interface tutorial, record the application itself.

These videos are animated demonstrations, not footage of physically tested builds. Practical guides and video steps remain subject to actual build testing before a fully validated public release. No project uses heat, chemical propellants, or pressurised devices.

Autodesk Fusion documentation: https://help.autodesk.com/view/fusion360/ENU/?contextId=SLD-EXTRUDE-SOLID and https://help.autodesk.com/view/fusion360/ENU/?contextId=ASM-CREATE-JOINT
