Sources, reuse and safety

Reference information for readers and future contributors.

Independent lessons

These are independent educational drafts, not MIT courses. The explanations, worked examples and browser demonstrations were developed for this website. The university links at the end of lessons point to further study. A reference link alone does not mean that its source's text, figures or exercises were copied.

Every lesson includes a subject-specific source link. Readers should verify calculations, model assumptions and important decisions against reliable sources. The complete curriculum has not received an independent subject-specialist review.

The lessons are free to read. Linking to MIT OpenCourseWare is attribution to a study resource, not permission to reproduce third-party illustrations. The website does not contain copied MIT course text or externally sourced images in this release. No blanket reuse licence is granted for the independently authored website material.

Reusing MIT OpenCourseWare material

MIT OCW terms identify a Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike 4.0 licence for eligible material. If we later adapt or reproduce a particular OCW item, we must credit the author and source, link to the licence, identify changes, follow share-alike conditions and check third-party rights. A free-to-view resource is not automatically licensed for copying.

Read the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence. No MIT affiliation or endorsement is claimed. Licence choices for the site's independently written material can be documented separately before release.

Safety and scope

Home activities are demonstrations, not engineering design or operating instructions. Use age-appropriate supervision and protective equipment as necessary. Do not mix unknown chemicals or household cleaners, operate energetic rocket motors, heat sealed containers or work with mains electricity as part of these lessons. Use paper calculations or simulations instead when an activity may be unsafe.

Idealised physics models omit real-world complications. Do not use this website to size, certify or operate safety-critical equipment.

Quality and corrections

These lessons are independent educational material, not certified engineering guidance. Worked calculations, assumptions and exercises should be checked before use in important decisions. Corrections and improvements will be incorporated as the lessons develop. The authors do not claim institutional endorsement or specialist certification.
