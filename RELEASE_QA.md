# Release QA — V9, 19 October 2026

## What is checked
- All **70 lesson pages**, four subject pages, 350 practice problems with nonempty answer explanations, and at least two worked examples per lesson.
- Internal links/assets, unique HTML IDs, language declarations, page heading structure and named controls.
- The subject directory, keyword search (including body text), expand/collapse, theme switch, keyboard and mobile browser smoke tests.
- Samples of numeric calculations from all subject areas; theory and equations reviewed for representative lessons.
- Home demonstrations screened for obvious physical/chemical safety concerns; activity cautions are visible on the sources/safety page.
- Source references point to MIT OCW as further reading, not endorsement. No external images/course PDFs are bundled. Site does not automatically inherit MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA licence.
- Corrected exact duplicate practice questions in Moles and Redox; differentiated a near-duplicate in Launch Loads.
- Keyboard focus appearance, reduced-motion and forced-colors fallback improved.

## Important limits
- **350 practice answers have been checked for presence and answer disclosure, not each independently certified for scientific correctness.** Review by specialists is still advisable before calling these authoritative academic resources.
- No independent expert certification, manual screen-reader test with NVDA/VoiceOver, full WCAG conformance assessment, or physical mobile-device test has occurred.
- Chemistry and making activities require reader care and age-appropriate supervision; no energetic motors, mains power or chemical mixing beyond safe kitchen materials is required.
- Any future illustrations or course-derived resources must have their own licences checked and credited before reuse.
- Website publication is a decision of the owner; these files have not been pushed to any public repository by this build.

## Practical release approach
A public **beta** with clear attribution, safety and scope information is reasonable. Continue review and correction over time; do not claim institutional endorsement or full independent certification.


## Version 10 addition — project previews
Six user-approved project guides and a functional expandable directory are included for design review. Guides require actual build testing and editorial/safety sign-off before being presented as tested how-to instructions. Course content from V9 is unchanged.
